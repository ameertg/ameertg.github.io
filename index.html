Hi, welcome! I'm at the Inria centre at Université Côte d'Azur, where I work at the crossroads of linguistic argumentation theory and computational linguistics. I'm interested in how meaning and reasoning show up in the way we communicate, especially in conversations and group discussions. Most of my work is about building theoretical and computational tools that connect the everyday reasoning people actually do with formal reasoning.

Before this, I did my PhD at the University of Edinburgh with Nadin Kökciyan, on making the implicit parts of arguments explicit. Feel free to get in touch if you'd like to chat!
